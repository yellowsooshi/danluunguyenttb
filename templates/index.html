Alcohol Beverage Label Verification System

This system assists in verifying that alcohol beverage labels comply with regulatory requirements. Enter the product information as it should appear on the label, then upload images of the actual label for automated verification. The system will extract information from the labels using OCR and AI technology to compare against your submitted data.
